Prints the billed time entries on the customer invoice itself, on a page
of their own after the totals, instead of only the summed line ("7.50
hours of development"):

- grouped by task, with the date, the description and the time spent, a
  subtotal per task and the total;
- without a column for the employee;
- time entries without a task under "Other services".

Three levels decide whether they are printed, each one overriding the
one above: the customer, the invoice and the invoice line that billed
them.

Configuration
=============

- **Customer**, tab *Sales & Purchase*, *Invoicing of Time*: *Timesheets
  on Invoices* Yes or No, default Yes. The contacts of a company follow
  the company.
- **Invoice**, tab *Other Info*: *Timesheets on the Invoice* As on the
  Customer, Yes or No.
- **Invoice line**, optional column *Timesheets*: As on the Invoice, Yes
  or No.

Usage
=====

Invoice services based on timesheets from the sales order as usual. The
time entries billed by the invoice are printed whenever it is previewed,
printed or sent. The separate report *Timesheets* of the print menu
stays available.
