Show the work behind the hours

The billed time entries on the invoice itself, grouped by task, with subtotals

What it prints

  • A page of its own after the lines and totals, instead of only the summed line ("7.50 hours of development").
  • Grouped by task: date, description and time spent, a subtotal per task and the total.
  • No column for the employee; time entries without a task under "Other services".
  • Three levels decide, each one overriding the one above: the customer, the invoice and the invoice line that billed the time entries.
  • The contacts of a company follow the company.
  • Preview, print and mail of the invoice all contain it.

Switch it per invoice

As on the customer, yes or no – and per invoice line in an optional column

Good to know

  • Requires Sales and Timesheets (sale_timesheet).
  • The separate report "Timesheets" of the print menu stays available.
